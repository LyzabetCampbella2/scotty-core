# SCOTTY tailored chief and specialist workspaces — development prototype

## Scope
- 25 distinct chief personalities and domain-based job menus.
- 160 specialist profiles, each with an individual ID, domain job, one of ten working styles, and a proposed reviewing chief.
- Proposed project categories: SCOTTY AIOS, Eirden Atelier, Creative Writing Projects, Education & Personal Curriculum, Creative Business & Content. These are **category templates**, not retrieved or connected private project records.
- Project recommendations, owner-approved assignment proposals, project-filtered biography fields for tasks/questions/missions/collaborators, evidence-based knowledge update proposals, specialist → chief → SCOTTY → owner review chain.

## Safety and limits
No sensitive user details are embedded. Project labels and needs are general templates, not real authorized data. Knowledge is **not** auto-updated from the web. No autonomous execution, actual model reasoning, live memory, voice, dashboard UI, live project sync, backend persistence or messaging is included. Test actor authorization is injected, not independently verified. Do not use these functions in production without trusted server-side identity and project-level authorization.
All assignments and knowledge changes remain proposals requiring owner approval. No production modification, deployment, paid resources or secrets.
